So we're eating pizza...

Ordering pizza is probably one of the most common things you'll do with a group. As a vegan, this is particularly tricky. Not every chain has vegan options, and sometimes it's a fat fucking lie.

So this is what I do when i need to order pizza...

New York Pizza

My preferred choice: They have two options, but technically more if you customize your toppings a lil. Plus they're pretty much everywhere.

Frozen Pizza

Decent option, if you can find it...

Domino's Pizza

Last resort. Their pizza is dry and will give you diarrhea. But if you must...

Order the Vegan Veggi option, or whatever has extra sauce. I don't trust their meat subsitutes.

Literally anything else

The following franchises all have a vegan option, and usually that one is the best one.

  • Pazzi Amsterdam - Vegana
  • De Pizzabakkers - Marinara
  • L'Osteria - Forza (vegan)
  • Sugo- Redefine Pulled Pork, Champignon-truffel & Parmezaan, Arrabbiata
