Climate Clock

The Climate Clock merges scientific legitimacy and cultural power to drive urgency, elevate systemic solutions and push policymakers to #ActInTime. The widget below is available for free.

I'm a big fan of Jiyi Guanli Yu (记忆管理局). Throughout the donghua (动画), Guan Chao ((关 潮), our protagonist learns of the Doomsday Clock and discovers the world is going to end in tomorrow through the doomsday clock.

I think it would be a fun challenge to try and recreate this doosmday clock's layout with pure CSS, and then give it a countdown using Intl.DateTimeFormat, as a stylized version of the Climate Clock using a 'shitbox' aesthetic.

I'll come back to this at some point to figure out how I want to build this.
