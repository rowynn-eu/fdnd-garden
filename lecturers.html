cool lecturers

there are cool lecturers on my campus.

while they don't always agree with one another, i still think they're cool.

this is how I view each of those lecturers.

she's behind the amsterdam-based queer kalendar, she cares a lot about others and making them feel seen. she's a huge inspiration and role model for me, personally.
