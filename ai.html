Views on Generative AI

TL;DR I don't use LLMs/Generative AI for writing, visual thinking or anything like that. Not for emails. Not for messages. Not for comments. Nor for anything that is supposed to represent me. These thoughts are my own, and this mindset extends to the work I create for my assignments, clients, or anyone I deeply care about. In this digital playground however, It'll be used (sparingly) to generate code to experiment with what AI is capable of doing as is expected during the second year of the programme.

I believe there are two kinds of people that use GenAI at my programme. Those that use it sparingly to generate code, but primarily to help get feedback on their ideas or to simplify complicated MDN pages down in plain simple english. It shows dedication that you're still willing to write your own code, and you're eager to learn how something works.

Then there's the second kind of person who recklessly generates code with AI and claims it as their own. Then, when confronted about it, they'll say that they made it, and they did it with "CSS" with zero explanation or thought behind what was actually done or why.

How I use Generative AI

I don't want to dependant on AI. What I care about is the act of making something, and not it having been made for me. With that in mind, It'll never be used to...

  • Write HTML/CSS/JS outside of this repository's ./ai folder.
  • Write, format or decide my own thoughts
  • Write the texts
  • Generate Lo/Hi-Fi designs
  • Generate design tokens

But I will say that I have used it in ways like...

  • Breaking down what is written on MDN in simpler terms
  • Use it as a search engine (like on duck.ai) to find sources/websites to read in plain english
  • Viewing and expanding automated generated summaries like on DuckDuckGo, Reddit and Google
  • Asking it something I don't understand because there as no mentor or teacher available to me.
  • Translating paragraphs English <-> Dutch

GenAI and Morals

I think to some it will come to a surprise that I'm willing to use GenAI/LLMs in this digital garden for generating code, especially given that I'm vegan and I identify with being an environmentalist.

They'll see my indifference to using AI as a 'gotcha', especially since data centres responsible for powering AI threaten water, land and climate.

I'm not really sure what to say to that. I'm part of the problem. No amount of eco-friendly sustainable choices will offset the harm I cause to the environment by typing in a prompt on duck.ai

Obviously, I dream of a world where AI is used ethically and powered by more sustainable powersources. But as of now? That world doesn't exist.
